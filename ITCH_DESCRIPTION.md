# 游龙坦克 / Yóu Lóng Tanks

A retro arcade tank column game inspired by Battle City (NES), with Chinese palace aesthetics and hand-drawn anime-style cutscenes.

## Story

A student walks out of a grocery shop with a bag of oranges. He sees a tank. He runs. The chase spills onto the city square — tanks and students everywhere.

As you progress, the resistance grows: students throw bicycles, build barricades, throw stones, make a last stand. 10 story cutscenes between levels tell the whole tale.

## How to Play

- **Move:** Arrow keys or WASD (left/right teleports across edges, top/bottom are solid walls)
- **Shoot:** Spacebar (from Level 3) — breaks barricades
- **Pause:** Escape
- **Menu:** Arrow keys + Enter (or mouse)

## Core Mechanic

Control the lead tank of a column. Ram pedestrians (10 pts), protesters with signs (20 pts), cyclists (25 pts), barricade-carriers (30 pts) to grow your column.

Starting Level 3, throwers send bicycles flying toward you.
From Level 4, carriers bring barricades from the bottom edge.
Hit a barricade — your lead tank gets stuck, control shifts to the next tank.
Entire column hits a wall or your own tanks — Game Over.

**Combo system:** consecutive hits within 3 seconds multiply score by +0.1 per step (5 in a row = ×1.4).

## Features

- **10 hand-drawn story cutscenes** (PC-98 anime style)
- **20 progressive levels** with escalating chaos
- **6 unique MIDI melodies** (茉莉花, 步步高, 金蛇狂舞, 花儿与少年, 红河谷 + menu theme 送别) via Web Audio
- **Combo scoring** with 3-second handicap
- **Juice effects** — screen shake, hit stop, particles, floating score, color flashes
- **16-bit pixel art** with gradients
- **Local high score table**
